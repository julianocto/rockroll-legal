Guitar Designer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Português (Brasil)

This policy explains how the app Guitar Designer ("the app"), by Materia Apps, handles data. The app lets you design an electric guitar from parts, paint and graphics, and save, export and share it. It needs no account.

1. Your designs stay on your phone

The guitars you design and save are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them. You can delete a saved design in the app, or everything by uninstalling the app. When you export an image, it is saved to your phone's gallery (if you choose to save it) or sent only to the app or person you pick in the share sheet. A design code you share holds only the guitar's parts, colours and graphics, and goes only where you send it.

2. Disabled-parts list

When it starts, the app downloads a small public file from julianocto.github.io (hosted on GitHub Pages) with the list of parts and presets that are turned off. The app sends no personal data and no design data with this request. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information to deliver the file; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or keep these request details.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, app interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads never receive your designs. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first (Google's consent form), and you can change your choice at any time in the app: home screen, menu (⋮) > Privacy options. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in Android settings.

4. Purchases

The one-time "Guitar Designer Pro" purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is owned, to remove ads and unlock the Pro parts.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Your rights (GDPR, LGPD)

We keep no personal data on our own servers, so we hold no data of yours to access, correct or delete. For data Google handles for ads, use Google's options above. Questions: use the email below.

7. Names and shapes

The app is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any guitar maker or artist, and it uses no brand, model or artist names or logos.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
