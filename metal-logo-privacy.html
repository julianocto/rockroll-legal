Metal Logo Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Metal Logo Maker app ("the app") by Materia Apps handles data. The app turns a name you type into a metal or rock style logo. No account is needed.

1. Data that stays on your device

The names you type and the logos you make ("My logos") are stored only on your phone. Logos are drawn on your phone; we do not receive your names or logos. You can delete them in the app (long press a logo) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Photos and gallery

The app only asks for gallery access to save the logos you make. It does not read or upload your photos. When you share a logo, it goes only to the app you choose.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EU and UK the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time under About > Ad privacy. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

5. Your rights (GDPR, LGPD)

We do not keep personal data on our own servers, so we hold no data of yours to access, correct or delete. For data Google processes for ads, use Google's options listed above. For questions, contact us at the email below.

6. Names and trademarks

Styles in the app are generic genre styles and are not affiliated with any band. You are responsible for having the rights to the names you type.
