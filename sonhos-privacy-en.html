Privacy Policy of Dream Meaning & Dream Journal

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Português

This policy explains how the app Dream Meaning & Dream Journal (in Portuguese "Sonhos: Significado e Diário", "the app"), by Materia Apps, handles data. The app has a dream symbol dictionary that works offline, a dream journal and, as an optional extra, a short reading made by artificial intelligence (AI). The app has no account or login. It is for adults 18 and over only.

1. Your journal stays on your phone

The dreams you write, drafts and saved readings are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no sync and no server copy. We do not receive your journal. You can delete a dream in the app, or everything by uninstalling the app. Export creates a file on your phone that goes only where you choose. The dictionary is built into the app and does not send what you search.

2. AI reading (only when you ask)

When you tap "Interpret", and only then, the app sends:

  • the text of that dream and the app language, to create the reading;
  • a random identifier the app creates at install (not the advertising ID or any hardware ID), to count the daily reading limit;
  • a Google Play Integrity token, to confirm the request comes from the original app;
  • if you bought Premium, the purchase token, to confirm the purchase with Google Play.

This data goes to our server on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers), which handles it like this:

  • Dream text and language go to Google's Gemini API (paid tier), which writes the reading. We send Google no identifier of you or your device. Our server does not store or log the dream text. Google keeps prompts and responses for 55 days for abuse monitoring, as described in the Gemini API usage policies, and may have staff review flagged content. On the paid tier, Google does not use this data to train its models.
  • Random identifier: the server keeps only a cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of it with the day's count. That count is deleted automatically within 2 days.
  • Play Integrity token is checked with Google and not stored.
  • Purchase token is checked with Google Play and not stored.

The reading comes back to the app and is saved only on your phone, with the dream. You can save any dream without asking for a reading. If the text mentions suicide, self-harm or abuse, the app does not interpret it and shows support contacts instead.

3. Reporting a reading

Every reading has a button to report offensive or wrong content. If you use it, the app sends the reason you picked, the language, the reading text and the random identifier (not the dream text). The identifier is not stored with the report. We keep the report for 90 days to fix the service, then it is deleted.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads in the dictionary and, if you choose, a rewarded video for an extra reading. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ads never receive your dreams. In the EU, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy options for ads. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in Android settings. We block gambling and betting ads.

5. Premium purchase

The "Premium lifetime" purchase is made through Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. There is no subscription.

6. Adults 18+ only

The app is meant for adults (18+). We ask for age confirmation before the first AI use. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor sent us data, contact us at the email below.

7. Not advice

Dictionary meanings and AI readings are cultural and folk readings, for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, psychological or professional advice and do not predict the future. If you are in distress, please reach out: in the US, call or text 988 (988lifeline.org); in Brazil, call CVV 188 (cvv.org.br). In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

8. Your rights (GDPR, LGPD, US state laws)

We do not have your journal. The dream text is not kept on our server, and the daily count is deleted within 2 days and does not identify you. So in general we hold no data of yours to access, correct or delete. For data handled by Google (ads and the Gemini API), use Google's options above. Questions or requests: use the email below.
